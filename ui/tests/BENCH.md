## 7. Where the new architecture is NOT faster

* **First open of a lazy page.** A compiled-in page was already in core's bundle; a plugin page now
  costs an HTTP round trip to another origin plus evaluation — the COLD number above (8.0 ms)
  instead of ~0. `load: 'lazy'` moves that cost to the first `page:open` of that resource.
* **Every extra resource adds a fetch.** Ten plugins are ten modules and ten stylesheets, requested in
  parallel but never bundled together; the monolith paid for them once, in one file, even for the 90 %
  of players who never open the page.
* **A restart is not free.** A same-build restart re-runs `setup` and remounts every open page of that
  resource (the WARM number, 2.8 ms); the monolith had no such event at all.
* **Patches stop being cheaper once the delta is big.** One op costs 0.007 ms to apply and
  re-renders one child; a whole snapshot costs 0.500 ms and re-renders all 200. The two meet at
  about **67 ops** (0.500 ms / 0.007 ms) — below that a patch wins on apply time as well as on
  bytes, above it the snapshot is the cheaper message. §38.10 caps a flush at 64 ops and falls back to a
  `page:open` for exactly this reason.
